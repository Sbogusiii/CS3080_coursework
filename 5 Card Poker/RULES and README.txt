JACKS OR BETTER VIDEO POKER:

The goal of the game is to end up with a five-card hand that matches one of the winning combinations listed on the paytable, with the lowest winning hand being a Pair of Jacks or Better (meaning a pair of Jacks, Queens, Kings, or Aces).


The game uses standard poker hand rankings. The payouts are multiplier-based, meaning the score you earn is multiplied by the number of coins you bet (1 to 5). The hands, from highest to lowest, are:

    Royal Flush (2000x): Ten, Jack, Queen, King, and Ace, all of the same suit.

    Straight Flush (250x): Five consecutive cards, all of the same suit (e.g., 5, 6, 7, 8, 9 of Hearts).

    Four of a Kind (125x): Four cards of the same rank (e.g., four Aces).

    Full House (40x): Three cards of one rank and two cards of another rank (e.g., three Kings and two Fives).

    Flush (25x): Five cards, all of the same suit, but not in sequence.

    Straight (20x): Five cards in consecutive rank order, but not all of the same suit (e.g., 5, 6, 7, 8, 9 of mixed suits).

    Three of a Kind (15x): Three cards of the same rank.

    Two Pair (10x): Two separate pairs (e.g., two Queens and two Fours).

    Jacks or Better (5x): Any pair of Jacks, Queens, Kings, or Aces.

1. The Bet Phase

    You start each round by choosing how many coins you want to bet, from 1 coin up to a maximum of 5 coins.

    Pressing "Bet One Coin" increases your bet by 1 and subtracts 1 coin from your Bank.

    Pressing "Bet Max" automatically bets the maximum of 5 coins (or whatever remains in your bank, up to 5).

    The DEAL button only activates once you have placed a bet greater than zero.

2. The Deal Phase

    Once you press DEAL, the game draws 5 cards from a shuffled 52-card deck and displays them face-up.

    This is where your first decision is made: You must decide which cards to HOLD and which to DISCARD (replace).

    Below each card is a button that toggles between HELD and DISCARD. You can hold any number of cards (0 to 5).

3. The Draw & Score Phase

    When you are satisfied with your holds, you press the DRAW button (which replaced the DEAL button).

    All cards you did not hold are discarded and replaced with new cards drawn from the remaining deck.

    Your final 5-card hand is displayed.

    The script then calculates the score based on the paytable above.

    If you have a winning hand, your winnings (Score Multiplier × Bet Amount) are added back to your Bank.

    The game resets, your bet is set back to 0, and you return to the Bet Phase to start a new hand.
